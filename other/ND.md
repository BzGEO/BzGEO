# Miscellaneous facts about the University of Notre Dame

* The full name of the university is the [***University of Notre Dame du Lac***](https://www.nd.edu/about/history/) (i.e., the University of Our Lady of the Lake)
* According to the [April 2026 Campus Map](https://www25.nd.edu/assets/641237/campus_map.pdf), Notre Dame has:
  * 179 buildings sitting on a 1,261 acre (510.3 hectares) campus
  * Over 1,100 faculty members
  * Approx. 8,500 undergraduate students and 3,700 graduate students
* According to US News & World Report in Oct. 2026, Notre Dame is ranked as having one of the [top 40 most beautiful college campuses](https://www.usnews.com/education/best-colleges/slideshows/beautiful-college-campuses?slide=34)
* US News & World Report ranked Notre Dame in the [top 20 national universities](https://www.usnews.com/best-colleges/rankings/national-universities) for 2027
* In the same way that an informal list of [public Ivy League universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Ivy) has been developed - in response to the [original Ivy League universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivy_League) - Notre Dame is considered the [most prestigious](https://globalcatholicresourcecenter.com/the-catholic-ivy-league/) of the Catholic Ivies
* Notre Dame's US $22.6 billion endowment makes it the [6th most endowed university in the US](https://www.collegeraptor.com/college-rankings/details/Endowment/)
